The rules at this table

Deal & build

Start with +, −, and ÷. Ante one chip, then receive one hidden number and two open numbers. After the first bet, take your last open number. Use all four numbers and every operation once, with normal order of operations and no parentheses.

A drawn × replaces +, −, or ×; you always keep ÷. A √ must be placed on a number. Special cards get a replacement number; extra specials in that replacement draw are discarded.

Bet & reveal

Check, call, raise, or fold. The lowest chip stack sets the betting limit. Lock your equation before the final bet, then secretly choose low (1), high (20), or swing. Swing uses two arrangements and must win both sides to receive anything.

High and low winners split the pot; an odd chip is discarded. One qualifying side takes the whole pot. If all swings fail and nobody qualifies, the pot carries forward.

Break a tie

Equal distance? High favors the highest number card, then its color: Yellow Giraffe → Grey Elephant → Orange Tiger → Pink Mouse. Low favors the lowest number, then the reverse color order.

Cards run from 0 to 10 in all four colors. This table uses four × and four √ cards, for a 52-card deck. You need five chips to enter another hand. The match ends when you cannot enter, or no opponent can.
